archive.aero

Every U.S. VFR sectional since 1930, on one timeline. 7,668 chart editions across 3,741 edition dates (September 2026), each georeferenced and stitched into one nationwide layer, so you can drag the timeline and watch airspace change over nine decades. It's a static site: tiles stream from Cloudflare R2 with no backend. I scan donated paper charts and publish every FAA cycle.
