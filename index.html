archive.aero 2025–now

A free, open-source viewer for every U.S. VFR sectional since 1930: 7,668 chart editions across 3,741 edition dates as of September 2026. Each edition is georeferenced and mosaicked into one seamless nationwide layer, tiled to PMTiles and served from Cloudflare R2 with no backend. Every conversion has to pass a 12-point alignment check within 0.3 px before it's published. New FAA cycles go up as they're released; older eras arrive as I find, buy or am given the paper charts. Dallas–Fort Worth in 1945 is a good place to start.

gdl90-esp32 2026

A bench simulator on an ESP32 that broadcasts GDL 90 traffic and weather over Wi-Fi, with offline browser controls over USB, so EFB apps can be tested without an aircraft or an ADS-B receiver.

M40 Scan

A macOS app for the Colortrac M40 wide-format scanner. Colortrac ships no Mac driver, so I reconstructed the USB protocol from their Windows driver package. It runs batch front-and-back scans from the archive's donation list.

Map tiling tools

geotiff2pmtiles, which converts the archive.aero mosaics to tiles, and pmtiles-rs, a Rust implementation of PMTiles.
